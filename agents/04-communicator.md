## The story (the one we tell everywhere)

> Ireland's property market runs on asking prices. Asking prices are ambitions. But the *truth* lives in official data — the CSO's national price index and the PSRA's register of every sale ever recorded. We put that truth in your hands, one chat message at a time. Ireland Property Catalogue: in line, above, or below the market — the numbers shown, the source named.

## The honest-claims angle (our moat)

Every public claim is framed as: *"anchored to official statistics (CSO/PSRA) rather than self-reported listing data alone."* That single sentence is our differentiation, our trust badge, and our regulatory shield. We do not say "this will save you €30k"; we say "here is the official market evidence — decide for yourself."
