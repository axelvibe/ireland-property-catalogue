⚙️ How the check works

1

Paste a live listing — e.g. from your Telegram channel — or just ask about an area.

2

The engine extracts price, beds, type and county, then compares against official reference prices for that county.

3

You get a verdict — in line, above or below market — with the numbers shown. AI adds plain-English interpretation.

📚 Sources

🇪🇺CSO PxStat RPPI — official price index & trends by region
🏛PSRA Property Price Register — actual registered sale prices
📲Telegram — live asking-price listings as they are floated
Ireland Property Catalogue — consumer-facing claims are deliberately anchored to official statistics (CSO/PSRA) rather than self-reported listing data alone. Reference prices are indicative county medians for guidance; always confirm with the PSRA Property Price Register before relying on a figure. 🍀
